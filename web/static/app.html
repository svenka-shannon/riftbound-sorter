Pump

After 60 s it switches itself off only if the cup rests on a station; otherwise it keeps holding (it may hold a card). "Off" works only with the cup resting at a station, or after you took the card off by hand.

Homing failed? Place the arm by hand

  1. Motor off: the yaw motor lets go and its saved position is no longer trusted.
  2. Keep the lift up and turn the arm by hand to the park pose (–, inside the home cam: the home switch reads pressed).
  3. Arm is at park, then Home.
